Aperture Watch App

ClientAperture
Year2024
RoleProduct · Art Direction
Timeline8 weeks

(Overview)

A focused wearable experience for capturing moments without friction.

Aperture needed a companion watch app that felt instant and intentional. We reduced the interface to a single gesture-driven canvas, prioritising speed and glanceability over feature density. The result is a product that disappears into the moment it's meant to capture.

(Approach)

Every screen was designed around the wrist — high contrast type, generous tap targets, and motion that communicates state without a word. We built a compact design system of tokens that scaled cleanly from the smallest watch face to the paired phone app.

Prototyping in motion early let us test the core capture loop in days, not weeks — and gave the team confidence to ship a version that felt inevitable.
