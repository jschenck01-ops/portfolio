01TreeRaiseBrand · Identity · Web

A giving platform that needed to feel inevitable.

The problem. A good idea — turn everyday shopping into planted trees — buried under a message that sounded like every other cause.

The work. A brand strategy and identity that put the emotional payoff first, backed by a website designed to make the act of giving feel effortless and obvious.

The result. A brand with a clear reason to exist, a coherent visual system, and a site built to convert intention into action.

02BlueDot CaresPositioning · Roadmap

Positioning that holds under pressure.

The problem. A home-care brand competing on the same promises as everyone else, with growth stalling as a result.

The work. A diagnosis of what was actually misaligned, a defensible position no competitor could copy, and a 2026 growth roadmap to execute against.

The result. A clear, ownable reason to be chosen — and a plan the team could run without me in the room.

03Sneakers4GoodBehavior · Targeting

Designing for the moment of decision.

The problem. A donation model asking people to act, without engineering the moment where that choice actually gets made.

The work. Behavioral architecture and sharper audience targeting that reframed the ask around real motivation, not obligation.

The result. A donation experience built around how people actually decide — and a model set up to scale.
