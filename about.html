About

I build the part of a brand a machine can't.

Chief Brand Strategist and Fractional CMO. Fifteen years turning misaligned brands into ones with a clear reason to exist — and the commercial results to prove it.

Most brands are producing more than ever, and saying less that actually matters.

AI made execution cheap. It handles production, distribution, and optimization better every month. What it cannot handle is the why — why a brand exists, what it refuses to be, and how it makes people feel when the transaction ends.

That's the work I do. I embed with senior leaders as a Fractional CMO, or come in on a focused brand-strategy engagement, and build the philosophical architecture underneath the marketing — the meaning that execution can't manufacture.

Over fifteen years I've worked across healthcare, EdTech, SaaS, entertainment, and nonprofit — with startups and enterprises, B2B and B2C — generating more than $20M through targeted campaigns and leading 130+ projects. I hold an MBA in International Marketing and work with clients globally.
