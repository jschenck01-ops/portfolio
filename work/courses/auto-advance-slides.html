How a Bridge Carries a Truck

Slide 01 · The load lands

A truck rolls onto the deck

Eighty thousand pounds now presses straight down on the road surface. That force has to reach the ground without the bridge moving. Follow where it goes.

TRUCK → DECK

Slide 02 · Bending

The deck hands it to the beams

The deck can't span far alone, so it passes the weight sideways to the girders beneath it. The girders resist by bending, ever so slightly, without breaking.

DECK → GIRDERS (bending)

Slide 03 · Compression

The beams press down on the piers

At each end the girders rest on vertical piers. The load is now pure compression, squeezing straight down the columns toward the earth.

GIRDERS → PIERS (compression)

Slide 04 · The ground

The foundation spreads it into the earth

The piers meet a wide foundation that fans the concentrated force out across enough soil or rock to hold it. The load has reached the ground. The path is complete.

PIERS → FOUNDATION → EARTH
