AI-Powered Creative Ode To Greatness

Ode To Greatness

Cologne Campaign, Where Legacy Meets AI Storytelling

Jeff Schenck · Chief Brand Strategist & Fractional CMO

Genre: UGC Campaign · AI Persona Development · Men's Lifestyle Content

Tools: ChatGPT · MidJourney · HeyGen · Kling 2.1 · Photoshop · Suno AI · Soundify AI

The campaign positioned the fragrance as more than a product, as a personal statement. Rather than traditional luxury advertising, the strategy deployed an AI persona demonstrating the cologne ritual as a daily confidence practice, optimized for short-form social content.

THE PROCESS

The creative direction centered on "power and intimacy." Key elements included: Persona anchors: "defined masculine features, tailored wardrobe, and urban loft settings"; Lifestyle vignettes: morning light, accessory details, and application rituals; Narrative tone: "bold but reflective, positioning cologne as a marker of self-respect and drive."

Creative Elements: AI persona design, muted masculine color palette (deep blues, charcoals, warm amber light), and UGC aesthetics featuring "handheld reflections in mirrors, close-up product hero shots, natural imperfections that grounded the luxury."

Scripts emphasized brevity with lines like "Greatness isn't given, it's chosen" and "Every day begins with intention."

Making Them Talk

Audio design layered ambient music with environmental sound effects, creating immersive depth through "faint city ambience, echo of footsteps on wood floors, and subtle atmospheric depth."

Bottlenecks and Breakthroughs

Challenge: Avoiding clichéd masculinity imagery and generic fragrance tropes.

Solution: Introducing "rawness into the luxury: slightly unshaven morning looks, natural light falloff across skin, handheld mirror shots with reflections." This balanced polish with authenticity.

Why It Works

The campaign demonstrated that "men's cologne campaigns don't have to choose between cinematic polish and authentic relatability." The approach delivered "legacy-inspired branding accessible to modern audiences" through AI-driven creative direction optimized for social formats.

AI persona campaign positioning the fragrance as a personal statement

Immersive audio design layering ambient music and environmental sound

"Rawness into luxury" direction that avoided generic fragrance tropes

Legacy-inspired branding made accessible to a modern audience
