AI-Powered Creative Porsche AI

AI Marketing in Motion

Porsche-Themed Case Study

Jeff Schenck · Chief Brand Strategist & Fractional CMO

Project Overview

Using Porsche as a fictional brand canvas, I created a high-impact marketing simulation using AI image generation, character prompts, motion graphics, and campaign scripting.

The objective was demonstrating how fast, flexible, and on-brand a luxury campaign can be with the right AI workflow.

Why Porsche?

The brand choice reflected Porsche's sleek visual DNA, aspirational tone, and luxury legacy as a testing ground. I built scenes that looked client-ready using AI-generated cars and cinematic angles to demonstrate rapid campaign creation without ever stepping on set.

AI Tools in Action

The project utilized: OpenArt.ai for visuals, ChatGPT for messaging, Hailou.ai for image-to-video conversion, Photoshop for retouching, Premiere Pro for editing, and Sintra.ai for storytelling structure.

Output Renders

Strategy prioritized generating static images first. Static image rendering is faster, more controllable, and significantly more cost-effective than video-first approaches.

Output Challenges

The work acknowledged AI limitations, noting instances where a white Porsche appears to reverse down the road and vehicles flipped orientation mid-sequence, underscoring that AI is powerful, it isn't infallible.

Results & Takeaways

Key findings emphasized: AI delivers premium visuals efficiently with proper workflow knowledge; marketers can prototype solo campaigns; visual-first ideation accelerates alignment; and critically, you have to account for limitations or it can be worse and MORE costly.
