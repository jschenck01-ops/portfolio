Campaign & Content Software & eBook AI

eBook & Software Marketing

AI Content Strategy in Action

Jeff Schenck · Chief Brand Strategist & Fractional CMO

I used AI tools to build a complete content marketing campaign for software and eBooks, from branded mockups to messaging and vertical video, proving what one strategist can do with vision and the right stack.

Project Overview

Just me, my toolkit, and the open brief. I built an entire visual story system from scratch using AI-generated mockups, cinematic brand environments, and automated content workflows. It's not a client piece. It's a proof of concept that shows what's possible when you blend brand strategy with creative tech.

Production Challenge

Going from text or just the graphics on the box to full video in a prompt is difficult for AI, not to mention the issues with text. I first tried to use the graphic box, and in an image it wasn't too bad, but the minute I moved that image to a video prompt the text on the box would get bad! Really bad.

Why Software & eBooks

I've worked with digital products across industries, and I know firsthand how hard it is to stand out in those crowded spaces. Software and eBooks are often marketed like commodities, but they're deeply personal purchases, tools for change, learning, efficiency, transformation. I wanted to see how I could visually represent that emotional context using AI.

AI Tools Used

  • OpenArt.ai for stylized product photography and surreal backgrounds
  • Photoshop for polish and color correction
  • ChatGPT for overlay copy and content generation
  • Hailou.ai for motion video creation

Output Renders

I always begin the process by generating a high-quality still image. Starting with video-to-text generation often leads to frustrating errors, increased costs, and wasted time.

Visual Storytelling

Each mockup had a job to do. I wasn't just creating pretty placeholders, I was telling stories with every environment. A desert scene suggests clarity and simplicity. A foggy forest speaks to inner growth or evergreen knowledge. A sunrise at the ocean signals peace, rest, and readiness.

Key Results & Takeaways

  • AI makes it possible for one person to build agency-quality content at speed
  • Environment-driven branding makes intangible products feel real and grounded
  • A single asset can be repurposed into dozens of micro-campaigns with smart framing

The emotional layer of marketing is what makes the tech stick, and AI doesn't kill that, it amplifies it when directed with purpose

More than anything, this showed me that good strategy doesn't need big crews, just vision, tools, and clarity.
