Capturing the Chaos: Live Shots That Rock

As a concert photographer, I worked closely with artist management and record labels to capture the essence of live performances.

My role involved not only photographing the energy and atmosphere of concerts but also editing the images for both print and digital use. I coordinated with management teams to schedule shoots, ensuring that the photos aligned with the artist's brand. After editing, I shared the final images with the label and artists for promotional use, helping them enhance their visual presence and marketing efforts.
