Brand Identity & Design Valet Custom

Valet Custom

Content Creation, SEO Optimization, Digital Marketing Strategy, Email Campaign Development, Social Media Marketing, Google Analytics Reporting, Paid Ads Management, Virtual Showroom Tours, Print and Digital Design, Customer Journey Mapping, Website SEO Enhancement, Lead Generation, A/B Testing, Sales Funnel Optimization, and Marketing Automation.

Jeff Schenck · Chief Brand Strategist & Fractional CMO

Overview

At Valet Custom Cabinets & Closets, I created engaging content, including virtual showroom tours and how-to videos, to keep clients connected during the COVID pandemic. Using past design projects, I showcased our high-end interior design work through interactive walkthroughs and blogs that highlighted our craftsmanship

Blogs & Showrooms

These blogs not only shared valuable design insights but also featured customer stories, offering potential clients inspiration and confidence in our work. The combination of video content and written how-to guides strengthened our online presence, keeping the business engaged with its audience and driving new leads.

Video Content

I transformed client project images into elegant video content, highlighting our designs and showcasing our white glove service to high-end clientele. These videos included testimonials from satisfied clients and video walkthroughs of stunning home showcases, emphasizing the quality and craftsmanship of our work. Through these visual stories, we provided an immersive look into our design process, helping to build trust with prospective clients and reinforcing our brand's reputation for luxury and attention to detail.

Social Media Content

For a top-tier brand like Valet Custom Cabinets & Closets, creating elegant and high-quality social media content is essential to maintaining its luxury image. I leveraged the company's vast portfolio of client work and images to craft visually stunning posts that reflected the sophistication and craftsmanship behind each project. By curating content that showcased the exquisite details of their designs, I ensured that the social media presence remained aligned with the brand's high-end reputation, engaging their affluent audience while highlighting the company's expertise and bespoke services.

Blog Content & Development

I focused on creating informative and engaging blog content that highlighted the intricate features of our high-end products, such as Murphy beds, sliding doors, custom-built pantries with unique functionalities, and dovetail drawers. These blogs were essential in educating potential clients on how our innovative designs could transform their spaces. By breaking down complex design elements into easy-to-understand explanations, I provided valuable insights that helped clients envision how these features could enhance their homes, reinforcing our expertise in delivering bespoke, functional luxury solutions.

Email Marketing

I implemented a highly effective email marketing strategy that included A/B testing, a three-pane design layout, and targeted content. By refining the email format and focusing on high-quality CTAs and tailored messaging, I increased open rates from 23% to 55%. The new design was informative, visually appealing, and aligned with the brand's luxury image, while the targeted approach ensured relevance for each audience segment. This data-driven strategy boosted engagement and conversions, enhancing customer interaction and driving sales.

54% email open rate and 16.5% click-through rate through precision audience targeting

Positioned Valet Custom as the premium choice in competitive luxury-trades markets

Visual case-study content that shortened the consideration cycle for high-ticket projects
