Online Courses & Marketing EdTech · Instructional Design

AI-hosted courses, built to hold attention.

Lecture films and short-form marketing for the course catalog, hosted by Augie.

Jeff Schenck · Chief Brand Strategist & Fractional CMO

Online education has a completion problem. Most courses are recorded slides and a quiz. Students enroll, drift, and disappear.

The work here is the opposite: college courses engineered so the next click always feels worth it. As the instructional designer on the catalog, I own the full production, the concept, the host, the lectures, and the marketing that gets people through the door.

The host

Augie is the AI professor who fronts the courses. One consistent voice and face who can teach an anthropology seminar on Monday and a construction-arts module on Tuesday without losing the thread or the brand.

One host across many disciplines is what makes a catalog feel like a school instead of a playlist.

The courses

Anthropology, hosted by Augie. Civil engineering and construction arts. Each course is a full experience, structured lectures, real teaching, and lessons that stick.

The marketing

A course nobody sees is a course nobody finishes. The short-form pieces pull the strangest, stickiest moments from the material, the pope who put a dead pope on trial, the man who could not stop eating, and turn them into hooks that earn the click.

Same host, same world, built to travel on the feed and point back to the catalog.

In motion

If you are producing online courses and the completion numbers are not where they should be, this is the conversation to have.
