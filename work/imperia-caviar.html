Brand Identity & Design Imperia Caviar

Imperia Caviar

Lead Generation, Content Creation, Marketing Strategy, Analytics Reporting, A/B Testing, Email Marketing, Paid Media Management, & Social Media Advertising

Jeff Schenck · Chief Brand Strategist & Fractional CMO

Marketing Luxury

I crafted a marketing strategy focused on evoking luxury and exclusivity through the use of gold and black design elements, both in visuals and messaging. The high-end, elegant aesthetic was carefully chosen to resonate with discerning customers seeking premium experiences.

By emphasizing luxury, quality, and sustainability in every campaign, we enhanced brand appeal, leading to a 32% increase in sales. This refined approach successfully elevated Imperia's market presence, positioning it as a leader in the premium caviar industry.

Social Media Content

I developed a high-end social media strategy, focusing on premium audience targeting through platforms like Instagram, Google Ads, and Facebook.

High Quality Images

I emphasized the importance of using elegantly framed, high-resolution imagery to capture the brand's luxurious essence. Each product was showcased through meticulously crafted photos that highlighted the rich textures and premium quality of the caviar. High-quality visuals were crucial in reinforcing the brand's upscale positioning, drawing in an affluent audience, and creating a desire for indulgence. These images played a key role in marketing efforts across social media, websites, and ad campaigns, contributing to increased engagement and sales.
