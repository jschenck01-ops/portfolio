The Creative Shift

A weekly argument for brands that want to mean something.

Short, sharp essays on brand strategy, positioning, and the difference between producing more and saying something. Read by senior leaders. No fluff, no funnels.

Every week I take one idea that's costing brands money — a lazy position, a hollow message, a strategy that sounds like everyone else's — and make the case for what to do instead.

It's the thinking behind the client work, written for the people who have to make the call. If you lead a brand and you're tired of producing more while saying less, this is for you.
